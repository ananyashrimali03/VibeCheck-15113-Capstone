Describe your process. At a high level, how did you go about creating this project?
I got the idea when I got distracted by jus t selecting the perfect background song while working and also I was bored on my existingplaylists. I started by putting claude on brainstorming with me. We discussed ideas on what is the problem, what could be the possible solutions. We discussed  multiple ideas and ways to make the project smooth and useful. Then I asked claude to build a project document in detail about whatever it thinks from what we discussed ad edited that. I used the document and our discussion to take it to cursor. I used cursor on plan mode and asked it t develop a detailed execution plan through back and forth prompting. After the plan was made, it was used to create a detailed spec. I then asked agent to use plan and spec to create the app. Then prompted for improvements. Used more agents for review and analysis.   
What AI tools and strategies did you use? What models did you choose, and were you using them in a browser chat, in something like Cursor, in the command line, etc? Did you use an agentic process like in HW8, or something else?
I used claude.ai opus 4.6 for brainstorming and throught process, cursors auto mode and opus 4.7 for code writing and execution. I used the HW8 process but included AI as a collaborator rather than doing the planning and initial setup 100% myself. One thing I adde from my end was creating a plan.md for a holistic execution plan.  
Why did you make the choices above? I used the plan.md in my HW9 and it turned out pretty well. I thought its a great efficient way to make the agents do the work in proper steps. I also realised while doing HW8 that having a proper thought from the very beginning helps a lot so used claude.ai to brainstorm in the beginning so as to make myself clear on what exactly and how exactly this has to be made.
